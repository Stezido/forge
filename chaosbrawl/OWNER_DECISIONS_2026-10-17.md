# Owner decisions — implemented 2026-10-17

These decisions were supplied directly by the owner in chat. Exact wording approvals are recorded as source before/after receipts in website PR #50; no proposal was treated as approval.

- Wedding Ring: attachment-bound two-creature bond; single-object-target noncopy spells and abilities only; copy targets the other bonded creature. Detachment/re-equipping ends the bond. Official printing preserved.
- Just Do it: floor(d20/2) actual land sacrifices; draw for actual sacrifices; optionally put only drawn lands onto the battlefield tapped; separate targeted reflexive damage trigger per sacrifice.
- Annoying Running Gag: count every spell of that name cast by its caster this game, including the current cast and excluding copies; reveal/free-cast on draw with four lands if able; shuffle only on resolution.
- Friend or Foe: choose one opponent to choose the mode. Each 20 receives four cards/six life loss; two 20s affect both. Critical results replace the ordinary comparison. Without a 20, opponent wins only with a three-point lead.
- Tavern Slot Machine: result 20 creates all three tokens; optional sacrifice creates a separate damage trigger; artifact tokens counted on damage resolution. Other bands and 3/T cost unchanged.
- Culinary Disaster: upkeep Pay 2 life, impossible with only one life; nonmana costs are preserved by upkeep modifiers.
- Rollercoaster: I/III distribute counters; II/IV move every original counter individually to a chosen creature of another controller, simultaneously. Newly received counters do not move again.
- Hofi: -2 requires a Food sacrificed on resolution; -6 starts a turn-long all-creature death effect before sacrificing Food and applying the debuff. Food-token life loss is counted separately.
- Moshpit: the owner's final decision supersedes immediate-only behavior. Establish the death effect first; every creature that dies this turn counts, including later arrivals. Then deal 2 to each creature.
- Gastronomic Downfall: use the existing native I/II distributions and III counter-removal/damage; no missing third effect and no native text edit required.
- Confusing Signals: choose the player while casting, without targeting them; tapped creatures still get stun, but only actual tapping causes draws.
- Cini Minis: Cini itself is sacrificed as activation cost; Milk is optional during resolution, after gaining 3 life, to draw three cards.
- Shady Herb Dealer: reduce generic mana or one chosen color; preserve nonmana upkeep costs.
- Distant Cousin Shotcrusher: “you may” makes the duplicate feedback loop stoppable; tolerance 3 retained.
- Source spelling fixes: Rake/Bucket Equipment, remove rarity words from Recycle/Always With Us subtype, Enchantment for Fermentation/Awaitening, Plainscycling/Plains, Ari the Ripper capitalization.
- Sofa Pizza: after its graveyard trigger, activated 1B return permission with normal priority until end of the caster's next turn; no sorcery restriction.
- New cards: preserve Maternal Instinct, Higher Protection and Haze-Fueled Frenzy's native identities/art. Maternal's approved text allows optional movement between any two own creatures, chosen on resolution without additional targets, independent of the hexproof target.

Earlier Last Duel, Numbing Mass Resurrection, Academic Quarter, Tactical Purge, Pumpkin Catapult and Youthful Inner Heat decisions remain implemented and tested. Ordinary matches retain upstream rules when Chaos Brawl mode is disabled.
